How Lowe works

You ask, in your language

Type, speak or take a photo. Lowe answers in your language: English, French, Arabic, Russian and seven more.

Lowe checks real sources

School questions are answered only from the school's official pages, read every night. Each answer shows where it comes from.

AI writes it clearly

Chinese AI models (DeepSeek, Doubao) translate and explain, tuned for politeness and for students. It works in China without a VPN.

Your data stays yours

Lowe stores only what it needs, never logs what you type, and you can download or delete everything about you at any time.
