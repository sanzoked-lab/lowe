Translate anything

Between any two of 11 languages, with pinyin. Type, speak, or take a photo of a notice. Adjusts the tone to the situation: teacher, bank, hospital, landlord.

✉️

Write to your teacher

Say what you need in your language; Lowe writes a polite Chinese message, with a back-translation so you know exactly what you send.

Ask for leave properly

Inside Lowe Classroom: upload → release → study → verify

Teachers upload their own slides; students open only what was covered, study it in their language, test themselves, and the teacher sees every step.

1 · Upload

PDF or PPTX becomes slides in the browser — up to 200 slides per lesson, no extra software.

2 · Release

Mark “covered up to slide N”. Students see only that part until the next class.

3 · Study & self-test

Students review with an AI tutor (explain, summarise, quiz me, ask) and take teacher-written or AI-drafted quizzes, auto-scored with explanations.

4 · Verify

The teacher sees slides viewed, quizzes, scores and tutor use per student — plus attendance, leave and an end-of-term CSV report.

How Lowe works

You ask, in your language

Type, speak or take a photo. Lowe answers in your language: English, French, Arabic, Russian and seven more.

Lowe checks real sources

School questions are answered only from your own university's official pages, read every night. Each answer shows where it comes from.

AI writes it clearly

Chinese AI models (DeepSeek, Doubao) translate and explain, tuned for politeness and for students. It works in China without a VPN.

Your data stays yours

Lowe stores only what it needs, never logs what you type, and you can download or delete everything about you at any time.
