How to get Premium

Open Lowe → Me → Premium

In the web app. In the mini program, the Premium card gives you the link.

Choose a plan

Month, semester or year.

Scan and pay

Lowe shows an exact amount and a short code. Pay with WeChat Pay or Alipay and write the code in the note.

Tap "I've paid"

Premium is switched on as soon as the payment is confirmed, usually within a few hours.
