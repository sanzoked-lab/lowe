Why Lowe exists

I'm Lowe (江树臣), an international student from Cameroon studying software engineering at Hunan Institute of Technology (湖南工学院). I speak English and French, and when I arrived in China almost everything around me was in Chinese: notices from the international office, messages to teachers, the bank, the hospital, and even the lessons themselves.

Translation apps translate words, but they don't know how to write politely to a teacher, where the school published the resit dates, or what to bring for a residence permit. So I built the assistant I needed, and shared it with the students around me.

"No student should miss an exam, a deadline or a lesson because it was written in a language they are still learning."

How Lowe works

You ask, in your language

Type, speak or take a photo. Lowe answers in your language: English, French, Arabic, Russian and seven more.

Lowe checks real sources

School questions are answered only from the school's official pages, read every night. Each answer shows where it comes from.

AI writes it clearly

Chinese AI models (DeepSeek, Doubao) translate and explain, tuned for politeness and for students. It works in China without a VPN.

Your data stays yours

Lowe stores only what it needs, never logs what you type, and you can download or delete everything about you at any time.

Questions

Do I need a VPN?

No. Lowe runs on Chinese cloud services and Chinese AI models, so it works normally in mainland China.

Which languages?

English, Chinese, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic, Russian, Korean, Japanese, Vietnamese and German.

Can Lowe be wrong about school information?

Lowe answers school questions only from the school's official pages and shows the source. When something hasn't been published, it says so rather than guessing. Always check important dates with your teacher or the international office.

Is my data safe?

Lowe stores the minimum, never records what you type in its logs, and lets you download or delete all your data. See the privacy page in the app.

Is it only for HNIT?

Translation, writing, live class translation and Chinese learning work for any student in China. School answers and notices currently cover Hunan Institute of Technology; more universities can be added.
